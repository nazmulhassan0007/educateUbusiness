## What they all do
- Stock photography and busy, template layouts.
- Prices shown "+ VAT", or hidden behind quotes and demos.
- Business buyers are sent to a demo, a quote form or a separate "teams" page.
- Big claims and badges (RoSPA, IOSH, star ratings) stacked in the hero.

## Where educateU Business can win
- A calm, premium page in a category full of noise.
- One clear all-in price per course, certificate included, with no quote or demo in the way.
- Team buying that works on the page itself: pick courses and learners, see the total, add to cart.
- Real proof from the CMS (client logos, learner reviews) instead of stacked badges.
